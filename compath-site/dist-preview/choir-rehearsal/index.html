Choir Rehearsal

Choir Rehearsal is a private rehearsal library for choirs. Songs, a track for each voice, PDF scores and YouTube embeds, for listening on a phone or a desktop.

What it is

The plugin adds a rehearsal library to a choir’s WordPress site. The library lives at the path /rehearsal/. Each song can hold voice tracks, a PDF score, and a YouTube embed per track. A player stays at the bottom of the screen, so you can listen while you read the score.

Public songs can be open to anyone. The rest of the library can require a login. That choice belongs to the choir.

Who it is for

  • Choirs that want the current repertoire in one place
  • Choir directors planning the next rehearsal
  • People with the role Voice Leader, who add songs, scores and tracks
  • Singers, including people with the role Singer, who listen to their own part

Features

Song library

A paginated list of songs on the choir website.

Voice tracks

Bass, tenor, alto, soprano and other parts — one audio track per voice.

PDF scores

Attach sheet music and turn pages in the built-in viewer.

YouTube embeds

Each track can be audio you upload or an official YouTube embed. The video stays visible.

Phone and desktop

The player stays at the bottom of the screen while you follow the score.

Private access

Rehearsal pages can be limited to people who sign in.

Singer and Voice Leader

Singer listens. Voice Leader manages songs. Administrator also opens Settings. These role names are never translated.

Play preview

In Pro, Play preview plays a voice track from the song editor before you publish it.

Lite and Pro

Lite

Free

  • Free on WordPress.org
  • Up to 4 voice tracks per song
  • PDF scores, the sticky player, audio upload, and optional YouTube embeds

Pro

€49/year

  • €49 per year, installed beside Lite rather than instead of it
  • Unlimited voice tracks, microphone recording, and search by title
  • Play preview and the PDF score inside the song editor
  • From version 0.5.0, WordPress updates Pro while the licence is active

Live demo

The live demo is a shared library. Public songs can be opened without an account. Order details and an optional setup on your own site are on the product site, so the price list stays in one place. https://demo.rehearsal.compath.ee/rehearsal

Languages

The product site can be read in English, Estonian, Russian, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Swedish and Finnish. Open it in your language from the links below. Role names — Singer, Voice Leader and Administrator — stay in English in every language.
