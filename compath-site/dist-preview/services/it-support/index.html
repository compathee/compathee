IT support and maintenance

We plan, install and then keep the everyday IT in order. The work is for companies and for homes, and it can be done remotely.

What we take on

  • Planning and building computer networks
  • Sales, installation and setup of office equipment, computers and other devices
  • Domain registration, web hosting, and setup of e-mail and websites
  • VoIP equipment support
  • Ongoing maintenance after the first installation

Companies and homes

A small office and a home can need the same kinds of jobs: a network that reaches the rooms you actually use, a computer that is set up, mail that arrives, a website that has a home. We do that work ourselves. We do not publish a response-time promise, because it depends on the job.

How to start

Write to us on WhatsApp, Monday to Friday 9–17, or use the contact form. WhatsApp Say what you have today and what you want changed. If the job is outside what we do, we will say so.
