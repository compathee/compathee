Plugin and AI agent development

We write WordPress plugins and we set up AI agents for a business process, including n8n integrations.

WordPress plugins

A plugin is the right shape when the workflow is specific: a private library, a screen your staff use every week, or a link to another system. We scope it with you, build it on your site, and leave it so it can be updated.

Our own plugin is Choir Rehearsal, a rehearsal library for choirs. It is the clearest example of plugin work we ship and support. The product site is rehearsal.compath.ee.

AI agents and workflows

An agent or an n8n workflow can collect a form, move a file, notify someone, or prepare a draft. Before anything runs, we write down which steps may proceed on their own and which steps wait for a person. That boundary is part of the job, not an extra.

How to start

Describe the process as it works today, including the tools already in use. Contact us and we will say whether a plugin, a workflow, or both is the smaller honest fit.
