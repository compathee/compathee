Consulting on launching on Amazon

We advise sellers who are opening on Amazon. The work covers the account, the listings, and the basics of advertising, including PPC. It is consulting, not a promise of sales.

What the consulting covers

  • Account setup and a practical order for the first weeks
  • Listings: the title, the images, and the text a buyer reads
  • Advertising basics, including how sponsored products fit a launch

What we do not claim

You remain the seller of record. We do not promise a search rank, a review score, or a revenue figure. PPC is one part of the launch conversation, not a separate ads-only retainer dressed up as strategy.

How to start

Tell us the marketplace, whether the account already exists, and how many products you want to list first. Use the contact form or call on a weekday.
